# Content and Interface Copy Guide

## Brand voice

JosHomes should sound:

- Clear.
- Calm.
- Local without forced slang.
- Helpful rather than sales-heavy.
- Honest about what has and has not been verified.
- Firm about safety.

Avoid exaggerated claims such as `100% safe`, `guaranteed genuine`, or `the best house in Jos` unless a specific, defensible meaning exists.

## Core message

Primary headline options:

- Find a home that is actually available in Jos.
- Fresh rental listings. Clear costs. Direct contact.
- See current homes for rent across Jos.

Supporting line:

`Browse recently confirmed rental vacancies, view useful property media, understand the expected cost, and contact the responsible lister.`

## Main calls to action

Home seeker:

- Search available homes.
- View property.
- Call lister.
- Chat on WhatsApp.
- Request an inspection.
- Save property.
- Report a problem.

Lister:

- List a vacancy.
- Save draft.
- Preview listing.
- Submit for review.
- Reconfirm availability.
- Mark as taken.
- Pause listing.

Admin:

- Approve listing.
- Request changes.
- Reject listing.
- Place under review.
- Suspend listing.
- Resolve report.

## Freshness copy

- `Confirmed today`
- `Confirmed yesterday`
- `Last confirmed 3 days ago`
- `Last confirmed on 17 July 2026`
- `Confirmation overdue`

Do not say `live` unless the data is genuinely updated in real time.

## Metrics copy

Preferred:

- `24 qualified views in the past 7 days`
- `6 inquiries in the past 7 days`
- `Views count distinct recent property-page visits. Inquiries are explicit contact actions.`

Avoid:

- `24 people want this house`
- `Thousands are viewing right now`
- Any fabricated countdown or urgency statement.

## Price and fees

Use a clear block:

- `Rent: NGN 850,000 per year`
- `Agency fee: NGN 85,000`
- `Legal fee: NGN 42,500`
- `Caution fee: NGN 50,000`
- `Service charge: Not stated`
- `Estimated move-in total: NGN 1,027,500`

Helper:

`This total is based on the amounts supplied by the lister. Confirm every charge before making a commitment.`

When no fee is known:

`No additional fee was declared by the lister.`

Do not silently hide zero, unknown, and not-applicable behind the same display value.

## Verification labels

Use exact labels:

- Phone verified.
- Identity reviewed.
- Business reviewed.
- Professional registration reviewed.
- Property access verified.
- Landlord authorization reviewed.
- Platform-inspected property.

Shared helper:

`Verification shows what the platform checked. It is not a guarantee of ownership, conduct, or a successful transaction.`

## Lister types

- Direct landlord.
- Agent.
- Caretaker.
- Property firm.
- JosHomes in-house agency.

Sponsored or in-house copy:

- `Sponsored listing`
- `Listed by JosHomes in-house agency`

Never present in-house inventory as an independent recommendation.

## Contact safety

Near the contact actions:

`Inspect the property, verify the lister's authority, review the documents, and confirm all charges before paying.`

For WhatsApp:

`Continue to WhatsApp? Your phone number and profile details may become visible to the lister according to your WhatsApp settings.`

For calls:

`Call the approved listing contact. JosHomes does not listen to or record the call unless a future feature clearly says otherwise.`

## Inspection request copy

Heading:

`Request an inspection`

Fields:

- Full name.
- Phone number.
- Preferred date.
- Preferred time.
- Message to the lister.

Consent:

`I agree that JosHomes may share these submitted details with the responsible lister so they can respond to this inspection request.`

Success:

`Request sent. The lister can now contact you to confirm the inspection.`

Failure:

`We could not send your request. Your information was not intentionally submitted twice. Please review the fields and try again.`

## Report flow

Button:

`Report a problem`

Reasons:

- Property appears taken.
- Price or fees are different.
- Images do not match.
- Wrong location.
- Suspicious or fraudulent behavior.
- Duplicate listing.
- Unsafe or inappropriate content.
- Other.

Helper:

`Your report helps us review the listing. The lister will not see your identity through the ordinary report workflow.`

Success:

`Report received. Our team will review the listing and its history.`

Do not promise a specific resolution time unless the operating team can meet it.

## Form validation style

Use specific corrective messages:

- `Enter the yearly rent as a number greater than zero.`
- `Select an area that the public can see.`
- `Enter the private address. It will not appear on the public listing.`
- `State whether any additional fees apply.`
- `Upload at least three usable property images.`
- `This video is longer than the permitted limit.`

Avoid vague messages such as `Invalid input`.

## Empty states

Search:

`No current listings match these filters.`

Supporting action:

`Try a wider area or budget, or save the search for future matches.`

Lister dashboard:

`You have not listed a vacancy yet.`

Supporting action:

`Create a draft and add the property details at your own pace.`

Saved properties:

`No saved properties yet.`

Supporting action:

`Save promising homes so you can compare them later.`

## Error states

General:

`Something went wrong while loading this page. Try again, or return to available listings.`

Unavailable listing:

`This listing is not currently available to the public.`

Unauthorized action:

`You do not have permission to make this change.`

Upload:

`The upload did not finish. Your listing draft is still saved.`

Rate limit:

`Too many attempts were made in a short period. Please try again later.`

## Safety page outline

- Verify that the property exists and matches the listing.
- Meet in a safe manner and tell someone where you are going.
- Confirm the lister's authority to advertise or transact.
- Review relevant documents and charges.
- Do not rely on a badge as a complete guarantee.
- Keep records of important conversations and receipts.
- Report misleading, unavailable, or suspicious listings.
- Seek qualified professional help for legal or financial questions.

## Footer disclaimer

`JosHomes is a listing and communication platform. Verification labels describe limited checks and do not guarantee ownership, conduct, availability, or transaction outcome. Confirm the property, documents, authority, and charges before paying.`
